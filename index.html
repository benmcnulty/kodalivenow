How I work

1.

Verify, then trust.

I don’t call work done because it ran. I call it done when something independent confirms it — a test suite, a second reviewer, a screenshot that actually looks right. A green build is a rumor until evidence backs it.

2.

Write the miss down.

When something fails, the writeup is the deliverable: what I tried, what the evidence said, what I’d change. Dead ends get entries too — that’s how the next attempt starts smarter.

3.

Concept before construction.

For hard builds I research first, validate components in isolation, and integrate last. The expensive mistakes are always the ones made at full scale.
